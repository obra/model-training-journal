This is evidence of improved likelihood on one narrow text distribution, not yet evidence of transferable reasoning or chatbot competence. The older 130,209-label broader language panel remains a separate historical result; it was not rerun for these checkpoints. The last completed 32-case task diagnostic remains 0/32 correct and 0/32 protocol-valid. Its output-interface failure prevents interpreting that score as an isolated capacity test. A proposed before-and-after format diagnostic stopped at CPU readiness and has no successful trial result. A standard zero-shot BLiMP minimal-pair evaluation is being assessed as a complementary grammar measure; it has not run. Neither BLiMP nor this CFR panel alone would prove the project's transferable-logic goal.

The frozen tokenizer, model configuration and evaluation population are shared across the charted checkpoints. Exact saved-checkpoint metrics and stage-local label counts are in the sanitized chart data; the passive SVG is reproducible with python3 scripts/build_v4_v5_cfr_chart.py.

Training a small model from scratch

The initial objective is a language model with roughly 100 million parameters. The entries follow a randomly initialized 97,536,768-parameter causal model from environment checks to a completed real-text pilot. They do not establish a capable assistant.

What those results establish

The initial byte-token diagnostic established mechanical execution on a tiny repeated corpus. The later real-text pilot completed 2M valid training tokens and reduced loss on a fixed 16,384-token textbook validation sample. This is narrow language-model evidence, not a conversational capability evaluation.

The corpus record documents a 26.91M-token training pool. The 100M-token continuation target represents roughly 3.7 passes over that pool, not 100M distinct tokens; completion is not claimed here. Book-level license assertions and bounded live checks have explicit limits, and no source text is published.

The revised planning-world entry records implementation tests and evaluation preparation. The scripted baseline's zero score is under methodological review and supports no claim of model superiority. Matched supervised-training comparisons and learned-policy evaluation remain future work in this snapshot.

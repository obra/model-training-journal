This is an AI-assisted engineering journal about the practical work of training a small language model from scratch.

## How the entries are made

Entries are curated from an experiment notebook and, where available, checked against machine-readable result files. AI assistance is used to carry out and document engineering work. The prose is an editorial record of that work, rather than a personal account written in the project owner's voice.

Each entry separates the question, method, observed result and limitation. Setup snapshots describe what was known at that time. A successful diagnostic does not imply successful long-run training, and a falling training loss does not establish model quality.

## What is public

The site contains reviewed narrative, technical configuration, measured results and public references. The complete notebook remains local. Private machine details, credentials, raw logs, internal conversations and unrelated workloads are excluded. Dataset contents and third-party text are not reproduced in these initial posts.

Source hashes identify the diagnostic revision and sample used in an experiment; they do not mean the corresponding code, checkpoint or dataset is distributed by this site. Links to external documentation provide context and can change independently of the historical record.

## Publication and corrections

New entries may publish after an agent checks privacy, licensing, factual support and the generated site. A hash-bound review receipt is required by the local publish command. A new or changed file invalidates that receipt. There is no automatic raw-notebook sync.

The [public repository](https://github.com/obra/model-training-journal) contains the curated Markdown, static-site builder and rendered pages. Corrections are made as normal commits so the public history remains inspectable. The site uses no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking, and requires no JavaScript to read.
